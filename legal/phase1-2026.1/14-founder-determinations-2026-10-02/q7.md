## The question

Is the mistake-spotter an "emotion recognition system"?

## The answer

**A** — No. It describes how a delivery sounds, not how the speaker feels.

**What it changes in code.** Nothing. The determination extends what
`02-power-score-classification-v1.0-DRAFT.md` §7.2 holds for the voice-confidence
composite to the speaking-error detectors (`services/detector_rollout.py`,
`services/verbal_cues.py`, `services/acoustic_cues.py`): each describes a
delivery (speed, gaps, endings, fillers, restarts), none a feeling. The band
labels were renamed to `delivery_signal_*` on 2026-09-17 for the same reason.
`02-power-score-classification-v1.1-DRAFT.md` §3b and §9 carry it. The gate
`DETECTOR_TRAINING_AUTHORISED` stays off until v1.1 is signed and the training
consent question for voice features is answered (training on audit answers over
voice measurements is voice-based training, which `13-training-consent-wording-SIGNED-2026-10-01.md`
does not cover: that wording is text only).
