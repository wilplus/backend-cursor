**Why the author line reads as it does.** A controller may decide their own
compliance; the record has to say who decided. It is not counsel-reviewed and
must not be labelled so: the `mlc2-bundled-consent-v1.json` defect this pack's
README warns about was exactly that, recording "the approved counsel
determination" where none existed. Labelling these counsel-reviewed costs one
email to an outside lawyer with the eight answers attached, not an edit here.

## The question

How long do we keep the coach's Yes / No / Can't-tell answers?

## The answer

**C** — They die with the recording. Delete the recording and the answer goes with it.

**What it changes in code.** Nothing new: `services/data_purge_registry.py` already
keys `error_presence_audit` to the Take (`take_session_id`, delete order 59),
so the coach's answer is deleted with the recording's Take, and a second entry
keys it to the coach so it also goes when the coach does. The retention row for
the schedule is drafted in `15-coach-blind-check-privacy-line-and-balancing-test-SIGNED-2026-10-02.md`
§3, re-using `06-retention-schedule-v1.0-DRAFT.md` §1's rule "Voice measurements
are never outlived by their source" rather than a number.
