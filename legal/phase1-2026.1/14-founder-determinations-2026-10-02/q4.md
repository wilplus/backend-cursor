**Why the author line reads as it does.** A controller may decide their own
compliance; the record has to say who decided. It is not counsel-reviewed and
must not be labelled so: the `mlc2-bundled-consent-v1.json` defect this pack's
README warns about was exactly that, recording "the approved counsel
determination" where none existed. Labelling these counsel-reviewed costs one
email to an outside lawyer with the eight answers attached, not an edit here.

## The question

Where does that switch have to be written down?

## The answer

**A** — Both Terms and Privacy, in a new version everyone re-accepts before the switch appears.

**What it changes in code.** `config.py` gains `PEER_SHARE_POLICY_VERSION`, the
Phase-1 policy version that describes the switch, `None` until it is published;
`services/lend_your_ear.py` refuses the share (`TERMS_REACCEPT_REQUIRED`) until
the speaker's current authorization is on that version or a later one. The path
is the 1 October one: Terms 3.3 and Privacy 3.3 (`16-share-switch-wording-DRAFT.md`)
registered as the next `phase1-…` policy version, re-accepted through the six
screens, then the constant named and `PEER_LANE_ENABLED` flipped.
