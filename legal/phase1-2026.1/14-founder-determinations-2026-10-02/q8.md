## The question

The coach's own sentences about your passage — does the speaker have to say yes before we train on them?

## The answer

**A** — Yes, same as the other three. A coach's words about your passage are your data, even with the passage stripped out.

**The founder's own words, verbatim:**

> Changed from B to A on 2026-10-02 to remove a contradiction: counsel's signed answer of 2026-10-01 already held, citing Nowak, that a coach's note about a speaker's passage is the speaker's personal data even without the passage attached, and that CONSENT_REQUIRED_SURFACES stays all three.
