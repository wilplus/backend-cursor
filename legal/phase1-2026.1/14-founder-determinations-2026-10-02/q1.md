## The question

Before a coach listens to a clip just to check our tool, does the speaker have to say yes?

## The answer

**C** — Change the promise first, then do it without asking — but give everyone an easy off switch.

**What it changes in code.** `services/error_presence_audit.py` samples a clip for a
coach's blind check only while the speaker's "Personalised practice" choice is on
at sampling time (`_still_permitted`, reading
`services/processing_authorization.py` through `services/verbal_cues.py`); a
speaker who turns it off is out of the pool from the next sampling on. That
choice is the "easy off switch" of this answer, so it needs no new control,
only the wording that says it does this (`15-coach-blind-check-privacy-line-and-balancing-test-SIGNED-2026-10-02.md`).
The gate `ERROR_PRESENCE_AUDIT_ENABLED` stays off until the new policy version is
published and re-accepted (see Q4 for the path).
