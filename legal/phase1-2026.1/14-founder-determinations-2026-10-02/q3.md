## The question

Can another user hear a clip of somebody's voice?

## The answer

**A** — Only with its own switch, per recording, off by default — and switching it off pulls the clip from both pools at once.

**What it changes in code.** The share was already per recording, off by default
and revocable (`voice_album_shares`, the view `shared_clips_live`, migration
`migrations/a_moment_may_be_lent_an_ear.sql`). Two things were missing and are
now in `services/delayed_measure.py` and `services/lend_your_ear.py`: the
delayed measure's pair clips reach a listener only while the original moment's
share is live, so switching it off pulls the clip from both pools at once; and
the shared entry and the pair's before clip carry one pair id so a set never
holds the same voice twice. Contract 29d records it.
