must not be labelled so: the `mlc2-bundled-consent-v1.json` defect this pack's

determination" where none existed. Labelling these counsel-reviewed costs one
email to an outside lawyer with the eight answers attached, not an edit here.

## The question

The listener's answer — is that the listener's own private data too?

## The answer

**A** — Yes, both. It stays tied to the listener's account, they can ask for it, and it dies with their account.

**What it changes in code.** Nothing new: `lend_your_ear_answers` and
`delayed_measure_votes` are keyed to the listener's principal in
`services/data_purge_registry.py` (delete with the account) and placed at
account level in `services/data_purge_project_scope.py` (a project purge leaves
them). A request for a copy is answered through the data-right path
(`services/processing_authorization.py`, `request_data_right`). The wording that
tells the listener this is in `16-share-switch-wording-DRAFT.md`.
