WillpowerLab — How AI is used here
Version 1.0. Effective 18 September 2026.

WillpowerLab is an AI system. Please read this before you record.


YOUR RECORDING IS PROCESSED BY AI

When you record yourself presenting, the audio is sent to OpenAI, an AI
provider, which turns it into a transcript. OpenAI is not authorised to use
your content to train its models.


THE DOCUMENTS YOU GET BACK ARE WRITTEN BY AI

Your Ideal Text is generated by AI from what you actually said. It is not a
transcript and it is not something a person wrote for you.

Your feedback is generated by AI as well.

Both can be wrong. AI can mishear a word, misstate something you said, or give
feedback that misses the point. Check anything that matters before you rely on
it. You are responsible for what you present.

Your Ideal Text is your document. It stays as you leave it. Later attempts may
suggest changes, and you decide whether to take them.


WE MEASURE HOW YOUR DELIVERY SOUNDS

We take measurements from the sound of your voice — how much your pitch varies,
how loud and how varied your delivery is, how fast you speak, how you pause, and
how your energy moves across a sentence. We compare them against your own
earlier recordings, never against other people.

We use them for one thing: helping choose which of your own sentences to show
back to you.

We do not give you a score, a rating or a grade, and we do not show one to
anyone else. We do not use your voice to identify you, and we do not guess your
sex, age, health or anything else about you from how you sound.


A HUMAN ONLY SEES IT IF YOU ASK

Your recording and your feedback do not wait for a person. If you ask for review
by a human coach, we will ask you separately before any coach sees your
material.


More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
