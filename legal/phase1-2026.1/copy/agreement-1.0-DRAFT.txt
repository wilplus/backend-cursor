Before you record

WillpowerLab records your voice and sends the audio to an AI provider, which
turns it into a transcript. Your presentation document and your feedback are
then written by AI from what you actually said.

AI can mishear a word, misstate something you said, or give feedback that
misses the point. Check anything that matters before you rely on it.

We use your recordings to give you your own coaching. We do not pool them with
other people's, and we do not use them to train shared models.

Your recording never waits for a person. If you ever want a human coach to
review a take, we will ask you separately before any coach sees it.

Agreeing records the exact version of each document below, so you can always
check what you agreed to. If we change any of them, we will ask you again.
