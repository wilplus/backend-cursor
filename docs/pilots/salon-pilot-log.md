## Where things stand (updated 2026-09-30)

- **Format agreed with Katie:** informal, one person at a time, WhatsApp. No
  formal emails or reports to her.
- **Framing:** a wellbeing / emotional-regulation *practice*, not sales
  training and not therapy. Sales are the side effect.
- **Testers proposed:** Katie, Dan, Levia (family), then Aileen (employee),
  then other employees, about 8 people in total.
- **Study shape proposed:** longitudinal, at least 2–3 weeks, a couple of
  recordings per week. The control group is a waitlist that starts a few weeks
  later.
- **Sales measure proposed:** average units per week, per person, of the 2–3
  practiced products, compared before and after and against the products that
  weren't practiced. Normalized per clients served where possible.
- **Stress measure proposed:** a one-minute anonymous survey before and after
  (e.g. PSS-4), kept outside the app. Nothing is shown to users as a score
  (AC-9).
- **Kickoff proposed:** a short video message from the founder instead of a
  live call.
- **Timing caveat given:** the app needs more exercises and training first. A
  Warsaw tester group goes first, so the product stays the same during the
  salon pilot.
- **Waiting on:** Katie's reply.
