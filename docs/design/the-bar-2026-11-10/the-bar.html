The bar

Two places, always the same: "+", and the one thing to do now.

Five rules

  1. "+" never moves. While you record, the bin takes its place.
  2. The middle is the one thing to do now. A black button records; a box types. Nothing else ever sits in the bar.
  3. Nothing asked: the middle asks. "Ask Kasia" is a button in a chat that takes recordings, a box in a chat that takes typing. In a speaking coach's chat it is "Record Take 2".
  4. The chat asks: the middle answers. "Record your answer" when the chat takes recordings; the box "Type your answer" when it takes only typing; when it takes only taps, the middle stays "Ask Kasia". Answers to tap sit above the bar, "Not now" under them. A photo asked for: the middle is "Add a photo".
  5. Thanks is one tap. After a person's answer in a chat without typing, three taps appear above the bar (🙏 filled, 👍, 👀); one tap sends it and they go. In a chat with typing, you type your thanks.

What the middle is

Words

Signed already

  • "Record Take N"
  • "Record your answer"
  • "Type your answer"
  • "Not now"
  • "Stop"
  • "Ask {name}"

Drafts for the founder's OK

  • "Record your question"
  • "Send a photo"
  • "Add a photo"
  • "Answer {name}"
  • "Asked. {name} answers here."

What this replaces

  • The three emoji in the bar (N483) become thanks taps.
  • N597 and N600, the box and the small mic on the right, give way to two places.
  • N603's image button on the right becomes "Add a photo" in the middle.
