What the AI voice adds draft for your review

Everything above this line is the locked design, unchanged. The AI voice changes one message on one screen: after a try that is not yet better, in the voice Exercise. Use Show the AI voice step in the panel to jump there.

What the speaker sees

  1. Not yet, AI voice ready: in place of the encouragement, one app bubble: "That was good. But what if you said it more like this?", the label "AI version of your voice" and the one player. The speaker taps play to hear it; nothing plays by itself. Then the locked "Practise again" and "Skip".
  2. Again, at most 3 times: the first AI clip after a try says "That was good. But what if you said it more like this?"; every later one says "Not yet. Listen to how you sound and how you might sound. Keep calm and try again." At most 3 AI clips per exercise, counting the Exercise screen's. The locked 3-try limit and move-on line stay.
  3. The video rule: the Exercise shows the library video only when it fits exactly (its main target was detected) and targets the same problem the severity picker chose. Otherwise the AI clip takes the video's place on the Exercise screen, before the first try. When you later record a video that fits, it replaces the AI clip.
  4. No question and no consent screen in the walk. The agreement is part of agreeing to use the app, and the AI voice is made in the background (from Take 1 unless you decide otherwise).
  5. No AI voice yet, or turned off in Data & consent: the locked screen, unchanged.
  6. The example fails its check, or ElevenLabs is down: no bubble at all. The speaker never hears an unchecked clip, and the walk is the locked one.
  7. Better, and the move-on line, stay the locked words. The machine still decides "better" by willfidence (+0.05).
  8. In the chat (lens off), the AI bubble stays in the thread under the encouragement, like every other step.

Behind it (backend only)

  1. Which voice exercise: pace, pause or stress, by normalized severity 0–1 against the speaker's own baseline; one at most, none when all are weak. Nothing of it reaches the screen but the listening line.
  2. The clip: the exercise sentence, same words, made with Eleven v4 from the speaker's clone, then checked: decodes, not clipped, the words kept, and the change really there (pace slower, a pause before the target phrase, the phrase louder and higher, never shouted).
  3. The second try is also measured on that one metric only (improved, similar, worsened, unclear) and stored with the before and after, for the future exercise picker. It changes nothing on screen.
  4. Labels: the AI clip is never a label and never enters a training set (L3).

signed 12 Oct New words

How the lens works

One chat, stored once

Every step is a message in one thread (lounge_messages), each with its author: you, the app (line bank), the coach, the rewrite (LLM), or the journey message. Nothing is copied. The lens and the chat are two views of the same rows.

Lens on (the walk): one step fills the screen, with big bubbles, ‹ › between steps, and built-in buttons. There's no message box, so the review stays fast.

Lens off (the Lounge): the same steps as normal bubbles, answered ones with your reply, the message box at the bottom.

When the lens turns on

By itself after every Take, on the first new step.

When you tap a bubble with ›: a moment, "Choose your helper words", the Clearer version or Exercise, a judgement, sharing, the end message, or the Ideal Text card. It opens on that step.

Practice, then judging: try → message → "Practise again" (up to 3 tries) with nothing in between. Then, once, a bridge bubble ("Now 2 moments from other speakers.") and as many judgements as tries (max 3), counted in the top bar. Each answer moves on by itself.

A step still waiting for you has an orange outline in the chat. Answered steps open read-only, showing your answer. Helper words stay editable: open them, change them, and the chat keeps the old words crossed out above the new ones (rule 5: every version stays in the history).

Backend: how a moment is picked

in

The Take

Audio + slides, through processing authorization, as today.

↓
1 · split

Sentences, per slide

Transcript → sentences, each with its time and slide. New: today the unit is a 75-word block.

↓
2a · sound

S, voice score

The 7 voice cues against the speaker's usual voice, 0–1. voice_confidence.py, unchanged.

2b · words

W, words score

Few fillers and hedges · fits the slide · holds together. willfidence.py, now per sentence.

↓
willfidence(sentence) = S × W 0 … 1 green(slide) = the sentence with max willfidence on the slide, if ≥ FLOOR (0.6) and ≥ 5 words moments = the top 3 green sentences, in slide order none above → the single best sentence, softer signed line exercise = the sentence with the lowest willfidence (never labelled) W < S → Clearer version (LLM rewrite, as today) S ≤ W → voice Exercise (library match, as today) practice try Δ = willfidence(try) − willfidence(original) Δ ≥ +0.05 → "Good job" (signed) · otherwise → "Practise" encouragement (signed), up to 3 tries

What changes in the backend

  • Unit: 75-word block → sentence.
  • Picker: 5 sound bands → one willfidence number with a floor.
  • Served label: confident / weak → green only. The exercise target is sent with no label.
  • Caps: unlimited → 1 per slide, 3 per Take. Rewrites: at most 1 per Take.
  • Willfidence goes from dark and per Take to live and per sentence. Same S × W.

Numbers

  • lock FLOOR 0.6 at launch, then calibrated so about 7 in 10 blind judges say 👍 to green sentences.
  • lock MARGIN +0.05 for a better try.
  • lock At least 5 words for a sentence to be green.
