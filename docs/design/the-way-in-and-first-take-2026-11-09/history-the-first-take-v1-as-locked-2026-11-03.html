The First Take

The first Take

A proposal, for your OK

Everyone's onboarding is their first official Take. After the acceptance screen, Will says hi, and his bar has one big black button. It opens the recording screen as it is locked, and the first-timer's questions are the slides. They answer each one out loud, slide by slide, so Will hears their vocal and verbal cues from the first minute. While the Take is processed, Will shows what he heard for them to check. Then comes the walk, and the setup carries on. The ringed button is the next tap.

    What this changes

    • The plan's questions (N319, locked) move onto Take 1's slides. "When is your deadline?" is asked as taps only if Will didn't hear a date.
    • Never 3's wording: "the plan's questions come once, before the first Take, one at a time" becomes "inside the first Take, one slide at a time". Before any other Take, still only the feelings check.
    • The setup's voice note (N378) becomes slide 1. Its card "Here's how others will see you" comes while the Take is processed.
    • The processing screen stays for every other Take. For Take 1 alone, its labels move into a thin row at the top of Will's chat, so the wait is used for the checks.
    • The recording screen itself does not change: slide, clock, Back, Next slide, Finish take. Only its slides are the questions.
    • The rest of the setup (the team, the agreement, "You're set") comes during the same wait, as one tap each. Nothing stands between the Take and its walk.
