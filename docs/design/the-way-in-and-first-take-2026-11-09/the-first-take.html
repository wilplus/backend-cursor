The first Take

Locked 2026-11-03 (N401) · pinned 2026-11-09 (N551)

Everyone's onboarding is their first official Take. After the acceptance screen, Will welcomes them in four bubbles (N492), and the middle of his bar is one black button, "Start your first recording". It opens the recording screen as it is locked, and the five questions (N462) are the slides, each drawn as the centered poster. They answer each one out loud, slide by slide, so Will hears their vocal and verbal cues from the first minute. While the Take is processed, Will shows what he heard for them to check. Then comes the walk, and "Record Take 2". The ringed button is the next tap.

      What this changes

      • The plan's questions (N319, locked) move onto Take 1's slides. "When is your deadline?" is asked as taps only if Will didn't hear a date (N453 item 3); after the walk it is never asked (N459).
      • Never 3's wording: "the plan's questions come once, inside the first Take, one slide at a time". Before any other Take, only the feelings check.
      • The setup's voice note (N378) becomes slide 1, "Your field". Its card "Here's how others will see you" comes while the Take is processed.
      • The processing screen stays for every other Take. For Take 1 alone, its labels move into a thin row at the top of Will's chat, so the wait is used for the checks.
      • The recording screen itself does not change: the top line, the strip, Back, Next slide, Finish take. Its slides are the questions, drawn as the poster (N462).
      • The rest of the setup (the agreement, "You're set") comes during the same wait, as one tap each. Nothing stands between the Take and its walk.
