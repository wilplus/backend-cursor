The journey

What version 2 changes

Your note: "this is a part that we apply to everyone ... it should be your first official take ... whatever questions we have for the first timer, we need to put them into slides."

  1. Will says hi, in one line, then one big black button. "Your first Take is five questions. Answer each one out loud, one slide at a time." The button is "Start your first recording".
  2. The first Take: the five questions are the slides on the recording screen, as it is locked. About you · Your talk · The room · Your one sentence · Your opening. No set-up: the questions are the deck.
  3. While it is processed, the labels sit in a thin row at the top of Will's chat. Will: "While your feedback is being prepared, check what I heard." Then, one tap each: "Here's how others will see you", the plan, "Which team are you in?" (only with no link and no code), the agreement, "You're set".
  4. The walk opens when it's ready. Its row on top turns orange. On Take 1, the walk works on how the questions were answered.
  5. Then "Record Take 2" for the real talk: the feelings check with its Skip, then the set-up (topic from slide 2, length, slides).
  6. Moved: the plan's questions in Will's chat (N319) are now slides 2 to 5. The setup's voice note (N378) is slide 1. "When is your deadline?" comes as taps only if no date was heard.
  7. Gone: gap 5, the two hellos. Will says hi once.

It changes locked rules (N319, and Never 3's wording: the questions come inside the first Take, one slide at a time), so it needs your OK. The step-by-step version is on its own page: The first Take.

The gaps, and how each is covered

What the map found in the code, each with its cover. The tag says what it needs.

  1. The default ring.To decideA new account gets ring 2. The plan's questions need ring 5, and the chats and the join link need ring 6, so today a new person sees only the empty Lounge. Cover: you set the default ring for the launch. This prototype shows what ring 6 sees.
  2. The welcome shows again after signing up on the web.To buildOnly "Enter the lab" marks it as seen, so someone who signs up at /signup meets it inside the chat. Cover: a signed-in person never sees the welcome. No words change.
  3. A join link needs ring 6 from the person joining too.To decideOtherwise the join is refused without a word, no chat is made, and they land in the plain Lounge. Cover: the link brings its own access, so whoever joins through it gets the chats.
  4. The setup isn't built.To buildNothing in the code yet: no teams, no codes, no voice-note card, no agreement in the setup. Cover: N378, version 3, slotted in as shown.
  5. Two hellos in a row.Gone in version 2The plan's "Hi {name}! A few quick questions before your first practice." is no longer in Will's chat: the questions are slides. Will says hi once.
  6. Signing up with no link and no code.Covered here"Which team are you in?" comes during the processing of the first Take, with Skip (N371). Skip goes on to the agreement.
  7. A guest who signs up after Take 1.To decideThey never get the plan's questions before a Take, and the setup can't come before Take 2 (Never 3). Cover: Will never asks them about a team. A team reaches them by a link or a code, or they tell Will "I want to add a team" and he asks the same questions.
  8. The join page says "Join".Covered hereThe signed "Join with your {company} account" (N335) needs a company's own sign-in, which is an integration (N378 item 7). Cover: "Join" stays until a company has one.
  9. No Microsoft sign-in.Needs your OKBuilt today: WhatsApp, Email, Google and LinkedIn, plus Apple in the app. RASP's people sign in with Microsoft. Cover: a "Microsoft" button beside Google. It is the app, not an integration. One new word: "Microsoft".
  10. Demo chats for a real newcomer.To decideOn the web, a ring-6 newcomer with an empty list gets two demo chats, "Sarah" and "Ariel", that aren't real. Cover: demo chats only for test accounts. A newcomer's list shows Will and "Add someone to chat with", as the iPhone app already does.
  11. In the iPhone app, a join link opens Safari.To buildThere are no universal links yet. Cover: universal links for willpowerlab.com/c/, so the app opens on the join page.
  12. The ask link has no page.To buildIts words are signed (N313), but /a/ is not built. Cover: the page as on the setup prototype.
  13. Coaches are still a list kept by hand.To buildCover: WillpowerLab's coach link, with the pass code inside, makes someone a speaking coach (N352).
  14. Two kinds of code.To decideThe community pass code after a Take ("+ Add a community with a passcode"), and a team's code (N353). Cover: one code. A team is a community, and its code is the pass code (N352 item 6).
