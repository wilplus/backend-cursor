The journey

What version 2 changes

Your note: "this is a part that we apply to everyone ... it should be your first official take ... whatever questions we have for the first timer, we need to put them into slides."

  1. Will's welcome, then one big black button. Four bubbles (N492): "Hi {name}, and welcome. …", what the first Take is, Wozniak and Jobs, where to record. The button is "Start your first recording".
  2. The first Take: five questions are the slides on the recording screen, drawn as the poster (N462): Your field · Worth sharing · Who it's for · A moment · Your one thing. No set-up: the questions are the deck.
  3. While it is processed, the labels sit in a thin row at the top of Will's chat. Will: "While your feedback is being prepared, check what I heard." Then, one tap each: "Here's how others will see you", the plan, the agreement, "You're set".
  4. The walk opens when it's ready. Its row on top turns orange. On Take 1, the walk works on how the questions were answered.
  5. Then "Record Take 2" for the real talk: the feelings check with its Skip, then the set-up (the topic from the plan, length, slides).
  6. Moved: the plan's questions in Will's chat (N319) are now slides. "When is your deadline?" comes as taps only if no date was heard.
  7. Gone: gap 5, the two hellos. Will says hi once.

Locked as N401 on 3 November 2026; N462 and N492 have since changed the slides and the welcome, and this pin draws them. The step-by-step version is on its own page: The first Take.

The gaps, and how each is covered

What the map found in the code, each with its cover. The tag says what it needs.

  1. The default ring.To decideA new account gets ring 2. The chats and the join link need ring 6. Cover: you set the default ring for the launch. This prototype shows what ring 6 sees.
  2. The welcome shows again after signing up on the web.Gone (N471)The first page is design B: no "Enter the lab", and a signed-in person goes straight to the acceptance screen.
  3. A join link needs ring 6 from the person joining too.To decideOtherwise the join is refused without a word and no chat is made. Cover: the link brings its own access, so whoever joins through it gets the chats.
  4. The setup isn't built.To buildNothing in the code yet: no voice-note card, no agreement in the setup. Cover: N378, version 3, slotted in as shown, without the teams (N471).
  5. Two hellos in a row.Gone in version 2The plan's hello is no longer in Will's chat: the questions are slides. Will says hi once.
  6. Signing up with no link and no code.Gone (N471)No link means the waitlist, and there are no codes: Will never asks which team.
  7. A guest who signs up after Take 1.Gone (N471)There is no guest path: nobody records without an account.
  8. The join page says "Join".Covered hereThe signed "Join with your {company} account" (N335) needs a company's own sign-in, which is an integration (N378 item 7). Cover: "Join" stays until a company has one.
  9. No Microsoft sign-in.Signed, not builtBuilt today: WhatsApp, Email, Google and LinkedIn, plus Apple in the app. RASP's people sign in with Microsoft. Signed with N401 (items 6 and 7): a "Microsoft" button beside Google.
  10. Demo chats for a real newcomer.To decideOn the web, a ring-6 newcomer with an empty list gets two demo chats, "Sarah" and "Ariel", that aren't real. Cover: demo chats only for test accounts. A newcomer's list shows Will and "Add someone to chat with", as the iPhone app already does.
  11. In the iPhone app, a join link opens Safari.To buildThere are no universal links yet. Cover: universal links for willpowerlab.com/c/, so the app opens on the join page.
  12. The ask link has no page.To buildIts words are signed (N313), but /a/ is not built. Cover: the page as on the setup prototype.
  13. Coaches are still a list kept by hand.To buildCover: WillpowerLab's coach link, with the pass code inside, makes someone a speaking coach (N352).
  14. Two kinds of code.Gone (N471)There are no community codes and no open communities: a person's link is the one way in.
