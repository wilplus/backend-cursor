The Bar, Clickable

Pick a scenario, or just tap in the phone: every button works. Both chats follow one rule. Your things are on the left, and the one thing to do now is in the middle. To say no to a question, tap "Not now" under it: a grey word on your side. There's no ✕ in either bar and no mic. You type only in Will's chat, and only when he asks for your own words; a chat with a person has no box at all. In a chat with a person, the "+" adds a question for them: it opens "Ask Sarah", and your question always opens the default presentation. A red dot means tapping it starts a recording.

Will's and Sarah's words are examples. The new words for asking someone are marked for your OK; every other word is already signed. The recording screens, the walk and Your projects are shown as plain stand-ins; they don't change.
