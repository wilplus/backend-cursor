Invita Site With Widget
Demo · invitaheadspa.com's home page (a screenshot from 28 Oct 2026, so its own links do nothing) with the WillpowerLab widget installed by one script tag. The popup comes by itself after two seconds; the pill stays at the bottom right. One typeface, white and grey.

One line before </body>, on the pages they choose

<script src="https://willpowerlab.com/w.js" data-key="ws_invita…" async></script>
  • The workspace decides what shows: the pill always, the popup once per visitor after a delay, on the home and treatment pages. Not now keeps the popup away for 30 days.
  • Both use the system's typeface, white and grey, so they sit quietly on any page. The words come from the workspace.

What stays off Invita's page

  • The form is an iframe to our domain in production: the photo, the number and the consent never pass through Invita's page or its other scripts.
  • Send posts to our public requests route; Invita's page learns only that a request was sent, for its own analytics.
  • The answer comes by text with a link to the answer page in Invita's name: the trichologist's voice over slides, the treatment, the nearest of the four locations with a date.
