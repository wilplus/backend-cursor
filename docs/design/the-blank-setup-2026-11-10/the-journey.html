After that, it keeps learning

  • Every answer you give teaches your models.
  • Every guess you fix teaches them more.
  • Every "Was this useful?" tells Will what to suggest changing. Will suggests; you Keep or Change.
  • You can change any part at any time by telling Will.

The building blocks

  • What people send: a recording, a message, a photo, a file
  • What they get back: an answer, their words tidied, what to do next, a mark
  • A model: one question, three answers
  • A ready model someone shared, paid in tokens
  • Taps people can ask with
  • Who answers: you, or Will with your check

Who decides what

  • The questions are always the same for everyone.
  • Will suggests the choices, fitted to your job.
  • You decide: Keep or Change.
  • If you ask for something that doesn't exist, Will says so and passes it on.
